    Creating Events

    Marathon-based events offer unique opportunities to bring communities together and provide exhilarating experiences. Our website allows users to easily create their own marathon events. Users can follow step-by-step instructions to plan their events, determine routes, and prepare exciting experiences for participants. With customizable options, we provide a platform suitable for all types of marathon events. Creating an event has never been easier!

    Participating in Events

    Our website serves as a bridge for marathon enthusiasts to participate in events and experience thrilling moments. Users can choose from a wide range of events and join the ones that best match their interests. With real-time updates and interactive maps, participants can easily view event details and select the most suitable ones for themselves. Participating in events is an excellent opportunity to gain new experiences and connect with the community.
